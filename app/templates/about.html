About Guru Lekhana Seva

Our Purpose

This platform is dedicated to facilitating the sacred practice of Guru Smarana and Lekhana Seva (the devotional writing of divine names and mantras) for devotees worldwide.

What is Guru Lekhana Seva?

Lekhana Seva is a traditional practice where devotees repeatedly write the name of their revered Guru or a given mantra with devotion and concentration. It is a powerful form of meditation that deepens one's spiritual connection.

How to Use

1. Register an account to track your progress.
2. Select a Guru from the Parampara.
3. Choose your target count (e.g., 108 times).
4. Begin your Seva using our digital typing or handwriting tools.

Important Disclaimer

This is an independent devotional digital platform created by devotees. It is not affiliated with, officially endorsed by, or an official publication of Uttaradi Math Peetham.
